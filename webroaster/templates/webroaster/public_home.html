What we protect

Security services built for daily operations.

Each service is designed for practical field control: trained people, clear supervision, documented incidents, and timely client reporting.

01

Manned Guarding

Uniformed security officers for access points, premises patrols, reception control, and visitor screening.

02

Mobile Patrols

Scheduled patrol checks, supervisor visits, shift coverage, and escalation support for dispersed sites.

03

Incident Response

Incident recording, investigation follow-up, notifications, and evidence-ready reports for management.
